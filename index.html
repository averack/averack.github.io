Fidget: Mazes of Boreternia

A cosy fog-of-war maze adventure for iPhone and iPad.

Fidget is a little villager with a lantern, and Boreternia is full of mazes. Every maze is new, hidden in fog and revealed only as Fidget walks through it. Find the exit, dodge the traps and explore ten biomes, all with one thumb.
