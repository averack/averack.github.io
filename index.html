Fidget: Mazes of Boreternia

A cosy fog-of-war maze adventure for iPhone and iPad

Hear ye, traveller!

Fidget is a little villager with a lantern, and Boreternia is full of mazes.

Every maze is new: drawn fresh each time, hidden in fog and revealed only as Fidget walks through it. Dodge the traps, outwit the slimes and ghosts, and find the exit, all with one thumb.

Explore ten biomes, from the Mossy Cellars to the Cloudtop Spire, hunt down Lost Trinkets for your Vault, collect scrolls of Boreternian lore and dress Fidget in clothes found deep in the mazes.
